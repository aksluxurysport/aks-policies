Returns & Refunds

30-day return window

You may request a return within 30 days after delivery. For ordinary change-of-mind returns, slides must be unworn, unused, in original condition, and include original packaging.

How to request a return

Email aksluxurysport@gmail.com with your order number and reason for the return. Wait for return instructions before sending the item.

Refunds and shipping

After the return is received and inspected, eligible refunds are issued to the original payment method. Original shipping charges are generally nonrefundable for voluntary returns, and customers are responsible for return shipping unless the item is damaged, defective, or incorrect, or applicable law requires otherwise.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

Contact us with your order details and photos if available. We will arrange an appropriate replacement or refund and cover required return shipping for an error or defective product. Your statutory rights are not limited by this policy.

Preorder cancellation

You may cancel a preorder before it ships and request a refund. See Preorder Terms for delay-related rights.
